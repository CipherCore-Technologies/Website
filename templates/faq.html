Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working with CipherCore Technologies.

01 What services do you offer?
We build websites, custom software, management systems, and portals for businesses, schools, and organizations. See our Services page for full details.
02 How much do your projects cost?
Pricing depends on the size and complexity of the project. Reach out with your idea and we'll give you a free, no-obligation quote.
03 How long does a typical project take?
Timelines vary depending on scope, but most small-to-medium projects are delivered within a few weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline before starting any work.
04 Do you offer support after a project is delivered?
Yes — we offer ongoing support and maintenance after launch. Details are agreed on a per-project basis.
05 Can you build something that isn't listed on your Products page?
Likely, yes. If it's practical software, tell us what you need and we'll let you know if it's something we can build.
06 How do I get started?
Contact us with a short description of your project, and we'll respond to set up a time to talk it through.
07 Do you work with clients outside your local area?
Yes — we can work remotely with clients anywhere, communicating by email or video call throughout the project.
