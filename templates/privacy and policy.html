Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

CipherCore Technologies ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website's contact form, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Any details you include in your message

We do not currently collect payment information, account data, or use tracking cookies on this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide only to:

  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Discuss and deliver projects you request from us
  • Maintain a record of client communication

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Information submitted through our contact form is stored securely and accessed only by CipherCore Technologies' founders. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

4. Third-Party Services

As our services grow, we may use third-party tools (such as email providers or analytics services) to support our operations. If this policy changes as a result, we will update this page accordingly.

5. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you
